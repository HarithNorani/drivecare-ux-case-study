## 5. Interview talking points

### “Why Innomotics?”

> I am interested in Innomotics because the products have a direct connection to reliable industrial operations and energy efficiency. This is a UX environment where clarity is not only a visual quality; it can influence maintenance decisions, downtime, and trust in the system. I would like to learn in that context and contribute a user-centred perspective.

### “How did you approach DriveCare?”

> I started with the decision the user needs to make, not with a collection of charts. The core question was how to help someone identify the most consequential issue and understand the next step. I defined three user groups, synthesised four initial insights, mapped the overview-to-action flow, and then designed progressive disclosure: calm summary first, context-rich alert detail second.

### “How would you validate the concept?”

> I would test the highest-risk assumptions with representative operators and maintenance technicians. I would ask participants to find the most urgent issue, explain why it is prioritised, and create or defer a work order. I would measure time to correct identification, prioritisation accuracy, confidence, and navigation errors. I would combine those measures with observation and follow-up questions, then share the findings with product and engineering stakeholders.

### “What would you improve next?”

> I would validate the alert severity model with real maintenance workflows and data, then test whether the recommendation language matches existing work-order processes. I would also explore permissions and different views for operators versus technicians. The current concept intentionally makes those assumptions visible so they can be tested rather than treated as final.
